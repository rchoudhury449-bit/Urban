For providers

Earn more by getting jobs from customers in your area. Urban sends you the work.

How it works
  1. Register with the form below.
  2. Urban verifies your details and calls you.
  3. Urban sends you jobs. At first you see only the customer’s area.
  4. When you accept, Urban shares the customer’s phone number.
  5. The customer pays Urban. Urban pays you after deducting the commission.
Rules

Do not take customers’ phone numbers or money directly.

Help

How to find a service
  1. Choose your village at the top of the page.
  2. Tap a service, for example Electrician.
  3. Tap Call or WhatsApp to talk to the provider directly.
Install the app

Install Urban on your phone to open it like a normal app.

App is installed on this phone ✔

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then ‘Install app’ or ‘Add to Home screen’.

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then ‘Add to Home Screen’.

For service providers

Tap ‘List service’ and fill the form. Your name and phone number will be shown to customers.

Good to know

Urban only lists providers. There is no booking or payment here. Talk to the provider and agree on the price yourself.
