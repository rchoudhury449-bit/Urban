Privacy policy

Who we are

Urban is a local service-referral service for Barpeta district, Assam, run by Rahul Choudhury (proprietor), Bhella, Barpeta district, Assam. Urban connects customers with independent service providers such as electricians, plumbers and water-can suppliers. This policy explains what information Urban collects, why, and how you can have it removed. The Urban app and website at rchoudhury449-bit.github.io/Urban are covered by this policy.

What we collect

From customers (only when you send a service request):

  • Your name, mobile number, the service you need, when you need it, and any details you type.
  • The village or area you choose in the app.

The request is sent as a WhatsApp message that you start and send yourself. Urban receives it on its WhatsApp number. The app does not read your contacts, your exact GPS location, your photos or your messages.

From service providers (when you register and while you work through Urban):

  • Name, mobile number, service category, description, village or area, working hours, languages and an optional price, given in the registration form.
  • Identity details such as ID type and the last 4 digits of the ID, only when asked on the signed provider agreement. Urban does not ask for or store full ID numbers.
  • Job records: the jobs received through Urban, the job amount, the commission due, due date, late fee, payments made and the account status.

On your device: the app saves your chosen village and language in your phone's browser storage, and keeps a copy of the app files so it opens faster and works with a poor connection. This stays on your phone. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Urban does not use advertising, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell personal information.

How we use it

  • To choose a suitable provider and pass on your request.
  • To share your mobile number and area with the chosen provider, only after the provider accepts the job. Before that the provider sees only your area.
  • To show a provider their own profile and commission account after they log in with their registered mobile number.
  • To calculate and collect commission, send payment reminders, and suspend an account when dues are not paid.
  • To call a customer to confirm that a job was done and what was paid.
  • To answer your questions, handle complaints and keep records the law may require.

Who else sees it

Urban shares information only as described here. It uses these services to run the app:

  • Google (Forms, Sheets and Apps Script) stores provider registrations and job records.
  • WhatsApp carries customer requests and messages to providers.
  • Razorpay processes online payments of commission. Urban does not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app and website.

Urban may also share information when the law requires it, or to recover unpaid commission through legal action under the provider agreement.

How long we keep it

Customer requests are kept only as long as needed to complete the job, handle any complaint, and meet legal or accounting needs. Provider details are kept while the provider is registered. Job and payment records may be kept for up to 3 years after a provider leaves, because they may be needed for tax, accounts or recovery of dues.

Delete or correct your data

You can ask Urban at any time to see, correct or delete your information, and a provider can ask to close their account.

  • Message Urban on WhatsApp on +91 88221 01577 with the mobile number you used and the word “DELETE” (or what you want corrected).
  • Urban confirms the request and acts on it within 7 days.
  • Customer details and registration details are deleted. A provider's job and payment records, and any unpaid dues, may be kept as explained above and are not deleted until the dues are cleared.

Messages already sent to a provider on WhatsApp are outside Urban's control, and Urban will ask the provider to delete them.

Security

Provider profile and account details are not public. They open only with the provider's registered mobile number, and the data sits in a private Google account. No method is completely secure, so please do not send Urban sensitive details such as bank passwords, OTPs or full ID numbers.

Children

Urban is for people aged 18 and above. It does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent information, message Urban and it will be removed.

Changes

If Urban changes this policy, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Your continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or complaints about your information: message Urban on WhatsApp or call +91 88221 01577. See also the Contact page.
