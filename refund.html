Refund and cancellation policy

Customers

Urban does not charge customers anything, so there is nothing to refund to a customer on Urban. The price of the work is agreed between the customer and the provider and is paid to the provider directly. If you have a complaint about the work, contact Urban and we will try to help resolve it with the provider.

Providers: commission and fees

  • Commission and fees paid to Urban are not refundable once a job has been completed and reported.
  • If you paid by mistake, paid twice, or paid more than the amount due, tell Urban within 7 days with the payment screenshot. After checking, Urban will refund the extra amount or adjust it against your next commission.
  • If an online payment fails but money is deducted from your account, it is usually returned by your bank or Razorpay automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If not, contact Urban.

Cancelling a job

If a customer cancels before the work starts, no commission is due for that job.

How to ask for a refund

Message Urban on WhatsApp on +91 88221 01577 with your name, mobile number, the amount and the payment screenshot. Urban replies within 3 working days.
