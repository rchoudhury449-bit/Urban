Terms of service

Who we are

Urban is a local service-referral service for Barpeta district, Assam, run by Rahul Choudhury (proprietor). Urban connects customers with independent service providers such as electricians, plumbers and drivers. Urban does not do the work itself and the providers are not Urban's employees.

For customers

  • Sending a request is free. Urban charges the customer no fee.
  • Urban chooses a provider and shares your phone number with that provider only after the provider accepts the job.
  • Confirm the price with Urban before the work starts. After the work, the provider makes a bill in the Urban app. Check the amount, then pay either in cash to the provider, or online through the payment link the provider sends you. If you pay online, the money goes to Urban, which pays the provider after deducting its commission.
  • After the job you can rate the work. Please give honest feedback.
  • Providers are independent. They are responsible for the quality and safety of their work. Tell Urban about any problem and we will try to help, but Urban does not guarantee the work.

For service providers

  • A provider must register with true details, and accepts the Urban Provider Agreement.
  • Urban charges the provider a commission of 10% of each job received through Urban, payable within 3 days of the job.
  • A late fee of Rs 20 per day applies after the due date (never more than the overdue commission). If commission stays unpaid for more than 7 days after the due date, the account is suspended automatically. Unpaid dues may be recovered through legal action.
  • For every job the provider must make the bill in the Urban app with the true amount. A false or reduced bill can lead to suspension and a penalty.
  • Cash jobs: the provider collects the cash and the commission becomes due as above. Online jobs: the customer pays Urban, Urban keeps its commission and pays the balance to the provider. Urban may set off any unpaid commission or late fee against that balance.
  • Urban may check any bill by contacting the customer. If the customer paid more than the bill, commission is charged on the real amount. The first time is a warning. If it happens again, a penalty equal to twice the missed commission is added to the provider’s dues and the account may be suspended.
  • Providers log in with their mobile number and a private 4-digit PIN and must not share it. Every bill made with the PIN is treated as made by the provider.
  • Providers must not take Urban customers outside Urban to avoid commission. The agreement explains the consequences.

Payments

Urban receives (a) commission and fees paid by providers and (b) online payments made by customers for jobs, which Urban passes on to the provider after deducting its commission. Online payments are processed by Razorpay. Urban does not see or store card or UPI details. Refunds are explained in the refund policy.

Changes and law

Urban may update these terms. Changes to a provider's commission or fees apply only after 15 days' notice. These terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts at Barpeta, Assam have jurisdiction.
